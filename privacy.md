# SoftKVM Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

## Summary

SoftKVM does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

SoftKVM stores the following on your Mac only:

- The monitors it has detected (name, vendor, product, and serial identifiers) and which ones you selected as triggers
- The names and Bluetooth addresses of the devices you chose to connect
- Your settings

This information stays on your device in the app's local storage. SoftKVM does not send this data anywhere, and the app has no network access.

## Bluetooth and notifications

SoftKVM asks for permission to use Bluetooth so it can connect devices you have already paired in System Settings. It may also ask to send notifications so it can tell you when a connection fails. You can change both permissions at any time in System Settings.

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or crash reporting
- No advertising or tracking
- No third-party services or SDKs that collect data

## Deleting your data

Use the "Reset All App Data" action in the app, or delete the app, to remove the information it stores.

## Children

SoftKVM does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
