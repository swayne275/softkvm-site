# SoftKVM

SoftKVM connects your Bluetooth devices automatically when you plug in your monitor.

If you share a keyboard, mouse, or trackpad between more than one Mac, SoftKVM handles the Bluetooth step for you when you switch your display.

It is also useful if you move your Mac between multiple workstations.

- Choose the monitors that should trigger a connection
- Choose which paired Bluetooth devices to connect
- When a selected monitor is plugged in, SoftKVM connects your saved devices
- If a device can't connect, you get a notification

SoftKVM has no account, doesn't use the network, and collects no data.
